## Definitions verified in responses

- Thresholds stated in every analyze result as `label_thresholds`:
  positive at or above 0.05, negative at or below -0.05, else neutral.
- `magnitude_basis` stated in every analyze result: "sum of absolute
  raw lexicon valences of matched tokens (per occurrence, before VADER
  rule adjustments), normalized to 0..1 as raw/(raw+15) using VADER's
  alpha=15 normalization constant". The positive sample returned
  magnitude 0.4828, magnitude_raw 14.0.
- Every analyze result carries `lexicon_version`:
  "vaderSentiment 3.3.2 lexicon", plus scores {pos, neu, neg},
  per-sentence breakdown, and top positive/negative terms with
  their lexicon valence values and occurrence counts.
